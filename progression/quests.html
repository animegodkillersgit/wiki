Quests

Quests give you a reason to come back every day. Do them for Gems, Hero Tokens, EC and Battle Pass levels.

A new day starts at 0:00 UTC on all servers. A new week starts on Monday at the same time.

World Missions

Each world has gate missions: defeat Recruit to Overlord enemies and the gate boss. Finish them all to unlock the next world. World 6 is the last world for now. You also get a permanent bonus from that world.

You get 5 daily quests every day and 5 weekly quests every week.

All daily quests give 175 Gems in total, and all weekly quests give 650 Gems.

  • Press Claim to get a reward, or turn on Auto to get it by itself.
  • Forgot to claim? The reward comes to your mail at the end of the day or week.
  • Battle Pass points count right away, without claiming.
  • Quests you didn't finish disappear when the new day or week starts.
  • A quest stays until the end of its day or week, even if you can't do it right now.
  • Each potion from quests is a random type out of 7.

Quest List

There are 33 quest types and 151 different quests. You get 5 different types at a time, and a quest won't come back until you have seen all the others you can get. New worlds add new quests.

Each goal is the same in every world, and every quest gives the same reward, so old worlds are the easy way. Quests count kills you get credit for. Raid enemies don't count for kill quests.

Login Reward

Log in each day to get the next reward of a 7-day cycle. Missing a day doesn't reset the cycle: you just get the next reward on your next login. You can't get rewards for days you missed.

Day of the cycleReward
Day 120 Gems
Day 21 S potion
Day 330 EC
Day 41 rune fragment
Day 51 M potion
Day 650 Gems
Day 7150 Gems

Press Claim in the reward window, or turn on Auto. If you don't claim it, it comes to your mail at the end of the day.

Codes

Codes give free rewards. See Codes for the active codes and what each type gives.

Mail

Your mail keeps rewards until you claim them, with no time limit. It gets:

  • Unclaimed Battle Pass rewards at the end of a season
  • Unclaimed quest rewards at the end of the day or week
  • Unclaimed login rewards at the end of the day
  • Gifts from the developers

Press Claim on a letter or Claim All for everything. If a pet, sword or accessory has no inventory space, the letter waits until you free some.
