Level & Prestige

Levels and stat points make you stronger for good. Rank Up doesn't touch them. Prestige resets them, but raises your limits and gives you a reward.

Levels

Enemies you defeat give XP, and XP raises your level. Boss Rush is the one exception. Extra XP is never lost. If you get enough for several levels, you gain them all at once.

Stat Points

You get 1 stat point per level, and more after each prestige. Put each point into one of four stats. The points in a stat add up and multiply that stat.

StatBonus per point
Energy+1%
Damage+1%
Coins+1%
Star Luck+0.25%
  • Turn on Auto for a stat in the Level & Stats window, and all free and new points go there right away.
  • Only one stat can have Auto on. Turning it on for another stat turns the first one off.
  • You can take back all your points at any time with 1 Reset Stats. Your level stays the same, and Auto turns off.
  • Reset Stats costs 29 Robux or 290 Gems. A set of 100 costs 999 Robux. The price never goes up.

Prestige

When you reach your level cap, you can prestige with a button. It never happens by itself. There are 7 prestiges, and the highest level is 330.

Worlds 1–6 don't take you to the level cap. Prestige and the Prestige Box come after World 6, so you can't get them yet.

Prestige puts you back at level 1 with 0 XP and resets your stat points. There is no "Are you sure?" window, only a warning line, so read it before you press.

You keep everything else: coin upgrades, achievements, Index, pets and swords.

Prestige Box

Every prestige gives you one Prestige Box:

  • 10 M potions of each of the 7 types, 70 potions in total
  • 5,000 Gems

Coin Upgrades

Spend coins on 11 upgrades that never reset, not by Rank Up and not by prestige. Each upgrade has a level cap that grows with the furthest world you have unlocked, so you can also buy levels while standing in an older world.
