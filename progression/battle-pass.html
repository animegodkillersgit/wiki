Battle Pass

The Battle Pass is a seasonal reward track. Every season lasts 4 weeks (28 days) and has 50 levels. When it ends, a new season with new rewards starts right away.

Seasons start on Monday at 0:00 UTC.

Earning Levels

Battle Pass levels only come from quests. Logging in, defeating enemies and playing time give no points.

  • A daily quest gives 30 points.
  • A weekly quest gives 150 points.
  • Every level costs 100 points.
  • After level 50, points stop adding up.

You don't need every quest. All the quests in a season give 7,200 points, and 50 levels need 5,000.

Season Aura and General

  • Every season has its own aura and general, a different pair on each track. They can't come from a gacha.
  • Season auras boost Energy.
  • A season general starts at level 1. Level it up with Hero Tokens, like any other general.
  • You keep them forever, even after the season ends.

Claiming Rewards

Tap a reward to claim it, or press Claim All. The Battle Pass has no Auto.

Buying Premium

Both purchases use Robux and last one season. You can't buy the Battle Pass with Gems, or buy levels one at a time.

  • Premium: 449 Robux. Opens the premium track.
  • Premium + 50 Levels: 1,999 Robux. Opens the premium track and all 50 levels at once. There is no discount if you already own Premium.

If you buy Premium in the middle of a season, you get the premium rewards of every level you already have.

End of a Season

  • Levels, points and Premium reset to zero.
  • Rewards you earned but didn't claim come to your mail.
