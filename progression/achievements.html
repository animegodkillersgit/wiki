Achievements

Achievements are long-term goals with permanent rewards. Your progress counts from the start of the game, and neither Rank Up nor prestige takes it away.

Each step of an achievement is called a tier. The raid achievements call them levels.

Press Claim or Claim All to collect a tier, or turn on Auto Claim. Unclaimed tiers wait with no time limit, but their reward doesn't work until you claim it.

Index

Index gives stars to every enemy and every pet tier in each world. Enemies earn stars from how many times you defeat that enemy, and pets from how many copies you hatch from eggs. Raid enemies don't count.

Every star adds to your Energy, Damage and Coins. The goals are the same for everything, even the rarest pets, and ★★★ is the maximum.

StarsDefeated or hatchedBonus to Energy, Damage and Coins
★500+2%
