Generals

Generals don't fight. Your one active general gives the same bonus to your energy, your damage and your pets' damage. Each general has one trait slot.

There are 6 generals in the general gacha, and every one has a fixed tier.

Season Generals

The battle pass gives a new general each season at level 50: a Champion on the free track and an Overlord on the premium track. They level up like other generals and never appear in the gacha.

General Traits

A new general has an empty trait slot. You fill it with a reroll, which costs 12 General Insignia. Runes don't work on generals. A trait works only while its general is active.

  • After a reroll, you choose: keep the old trait or take the new one.
  • If you roll the trait you already have, your Insignia come back.
  • The 15th reroll in a row with no Sovereign trait always gives Overlord's Aura.
  • The 40th reroll in a row with no Godkiller trait always gives a Godkiller trait.
  • Each general has their own pity counter.
