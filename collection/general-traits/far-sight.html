Far Sight

General Trait

Far Sight is a Veteran general trait. It only works while its general is active.

×1.2 attack radiusEffect
8.2%Chance per reroll

What it does

Far Sight Veteran

Multiplies your attack radius by 1.2.

How to get

Trait reroll

Spend 12 General Insignia on a reroll. Chance: 8.2% per reroll. Luck does not help here.

Direct purchase

Where Roblox blocks paid random items, you buy this trait for 90 Gems instead.
