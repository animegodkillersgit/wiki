Tactician

General Trait

Tactician is a Champion general trait. It only works while its general is active.

+10% crit chanceEffect
16.5%Chance per reroll

What it does

Tactician Champion

Adds 10% crit chance, up to the cap of 50%.

How to get

Trait reroll

Spend 12 General Insignia on a reroll. Chance: 16.5% per reroll. Luck does not help here.

Direct purchase

Where Roblox blocks paid random items, you buy this trait for 190 Gems instead.
