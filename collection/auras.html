Auras

Auras boost one or two of your stats. You wear 1 aura at a time, so pick the best one for the job. Putting on a new aura takes off the old one.

Auras come from three places: secret bosses, the aura gacha and the battle pass.

Boss Auras

The secret boss of each world drops its own aura (the World 6 aura is out of reach until World 7 comes out), with a 1% chance per kill.

Gacha auras boost energy or damage, and they are all stronger than any boss aura. The top two tiers boost energy and damage at the same time, with the full bonus on each.

Season Auras

The battle pass gives a new energy aura each season at level 25: a +160% aura on the free track and a +320% aura on the premium track. They never appear in the gacha.
