Swords & Traits

Swords add to your damage, but their real value is their traits. A trait can give damage, energy, crits, coins, drops and hits on several enemies at once.

You start with Wooden Trainer in your hand. Every other sword drops from the sword bosses of Worlds 2, 4 and 6.

Who Wears Swords

All swords come from one shared pool, so any sword fits anyone.

  • You have one hand for a sword. The Double Sword gamepass adds a second hand with its own full sword.
  • Every active pet can wear a sword too. It makes that pet hit harder.
  • Generals don't wear swords.
  • If you take a pet out of its slot, its sword goes back to your inventory.

Swords only add damage. They don't give energy by themselves, but a trait on a sword can.

Equipped swords earn XP from kills, the same amount you earn. They use the same level prices as you and stop at level 100. The damage of a sword grows up to ×2 at max level, and the first levels help the most.

Enchanting

Enchant a sword at the Enchant station in Raid Lobby. Each attempt costs 10 enchant crystals, on every star and level. Crystals come mainly from the Endless raid and also from the battle pass.

  • A sword goes from +0 to +10 and from 0★ to 5★.
  • Attempts up to the safe level always work. Above it, an attempt can fail. The safe level drops with every star.
  • A failed attempt costs crystals and drops the enchant by 1 level, but never below the safe level of that star.
  • A success on +10 gives a new star for free and resets the enchant to +0. At 5★ it stops at +10.
  • After a new star the sword is weaker for a while, until you enchant it back up.
  • Auto Enchant makes one attempt every 0.5 seconds, even while you walk away, and goes on through the stars.

Traits

New swords have empty trait slots. Slot 1 is open from the start. Slots 2 and 3 are unlocked with runes, and you put a trait in a slot by rolling a rune. See Runes for chances and prices.

  • Traits work from every equipped sword: both hands and all active pets.
  • The same trait can't be on one sword twice, but it can be on different swords.
  • Up to 3 copies of a trait count. Extra copies do nothing.
  • Traits of the same kind add up with each other.

Some traits count only once, no matter how many you wear: Tenth Strike, Berserk, Ricochet, Cleave, Chain Lightning and Salvager.
