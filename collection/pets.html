Choose Your Pet

Pets give you energy and attack on their own. Each world has its own egg with seven pets, one for each rarity tier.

Your first pet is free. On your first login you choose Pup Saiba, Leaf Toad or Crab Hand. You pick once, and all three have the same strength.

Pet Slots

You start with 3 slots and can reach 13 for free. The More Pets gamepass adds 2 more, for a maximum of 15.

  • Every tier of the Eggs Opened achievement gives 1 slot.
  • Tiers 5 and 10 of the Bosses achievement each give 1 slot.
  • You can equip several copies of the same pet, one copy per slot.

Eggs

You buy eggs with coins at each world's egg.

One opening gives 5 eggs and takes 3 seconds.

  • Every tier of the Eggs Opened achievement adds 1 egg per opening, up to 13.
  • The More Open gamepass adds 5 eggs, up to 18 per opening.
  • The Fast Open gamepass cuts an opening to 0.9 seconds.
  • If you can't afford every egg, or run out of inventory space, you open as many as you can and pay only for those.
  • Turn on Auto while standing at an egg to keep opening until you walk away. With the Remote Star gamepass, it keeps going after you leave.

Pity

Pity guarantees a rare pet after a long unlucky streak.

  • The 500th egg in a row with no Overlord is always an Overlord.
  • The 7,500th egg in a row with no Sovereign is always a Sovereign.
  • Godkiller has no pity.
  • Each world's egg has its own counter, and every egg counts, not every opening.

Energy and Damage

Higher tiers and later worlds make a pet stronger. A pet's energy grows ×2.5 with each world, and the energy of all your equipped pets adds up.

Hit strength is the share of your click damage (without crits) that the pet deals. Each active pet hits 1 time per second on its own, so you don't need to click, and pets go for the same enemy you do.

Your pets only attack while the Auto Pet button on the battle screen is on, and it starts off. With it off they don't hit, but they still give energy.
Keep your pets fresh: a pet's hit gets ×2.5 weaker for every world it is behind your furthest unlocked world.

Pet Fusion

Feed pets to a pet to level it up. The fed pets are gone for good, and stronger pets give more experience.

  • Pets go from level 1 to level 100.
  • A max-level pet has ×2 energy and hit strength.
  • Reaching max level takes about 500 pets of the same tier and world.
  • Locked and equipped pets can't be fed.
  • Auto-Feed sends new pets of the chosen tier or lower straight to Pet Fusion.
