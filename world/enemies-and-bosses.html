Enemies & Bosses

Every world has 7 enemies, one for each tier. The Sovereign is the gate boss and the Godkiller is the secret boss. Worlds 2, 4 and 6 add an eighth enemy, the sword boss.

An enemy always has the same HP, no matter how strong you are. Enemies stay in their spots on the map, and you choose which one to fight.

Respawns

  • Normal enemies come back after 3 seconds.
  • Gate bosses and secret bosses come back after 2 seconds.
  • Sword bosses come back after 10 seconds.

Gate Bosses

The gate boss is the toughest enemy on the way to the next world. You must beat it 5 times to finish the gate missions. It never hits you, so you can stay on auto-click.

Secret Bosses

Every world hides a secret boss somewhere on its map. It has as much HP as the Veteran enemy two worlds ahead, so come back when you're stronger. You can attack it at any time, and you don't need it to unlock the next world. It never hits you.

Each secret boss can drop its own aura. Once you own that aura, it stops dropping.

Sword Bosses

Sword bosses are found in Worlds 2, 4 and 6. They are not part of the gate missions. They are the only enemies that drop swords and rune fragments. Each kill can drop one sword at most, and swords have no pity.

When Bosses Hit You

Sword bosses and raid bosses are the only enemies that hit you. Everything else is safe.

  • You have 100 HP. It doesn't grow and doesn't heal over time.
  • Each hit takes 50 HP, so 2 hits will knock you out.
  • A circle lights up under you before each hit. Step out of it, and out of other players' circles too, and the hit misses. You can keep attacking while you dodge.
  • If you fall, you come back on the spot after 5 seconds with full HP. The boss keeps the HP it had.
  • Your HP refills when the boss dies or when you leave the world.

Fighting With Other Players

Enemies are shared by everyone on the server, up to 10 players, so you can hit the same one together.

  • You get credit for a kill if you dealt at least 25% of the enemy's HP. Below that, you get nothing.
  • Up to 4 players can get credit for one kill.
  • Everyone with credit gets the full reward, and each player gets their own drop rolls.
  • Hurt enemies don't heal, so you can come back and finish them.
  • If you walk away, your share stays until the enemy dies. If you leave the game, your share is lost.
