Ranks

Rank Up is the main way to grow. Every Rank Up multiplies your energy per click by 2, forever. Ranks go from R1 to R131.

How It Works

Every click adds energy to your stored energy, and your hits get stronger as it grows. When you have enough, you can Rank Up.

  • A Rank Up takes all your stored energy, even the extra. Your hits get weak for a moment, then grow faster than before.
  • Your rank never goes down, and prestige doesn't reset it.
  • Rank Ups give the energy boost and nothing else.
  • You don't need a certain rank to unlock a world. Worlds are unlocked with gate missions.

Button or Auto

Rank Up starts as a button. You can also turn on auto Rank Up, which does it for you as soon as you have enough energy.

  • With the button, you can keep saving energy past the price. Your hits keep growing, but your rank stays the same.
  • Auto Rank Up happens after your hit, so that click still hits at full strength.
  • The Rank Up window warns you that all your energy will be used.

At the last rank there are no more Rank Ups. Your energy keeps stacking and your hits keep growing.
