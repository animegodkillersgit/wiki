Bosses

Armored Elite (gate boss) Sovereign

Beat it 5 times for the last gate mission. It never hits back. Every kill drops 3–8 Hero Tokens.

Scouter Sentinel (secret boss) Godkiller

Hidden on the map and far tougher than anything else here, with the HP of a Veteran enemy from World 3. You don't need it to unlock the next world. It has a 1% chance per kill to drop the Luck Aura aura (+10% luck). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 4 Forge Core.
