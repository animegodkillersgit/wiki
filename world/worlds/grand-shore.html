Bosses

Warlord Guard (gate boss) Sovereign

Beat it 5 times for the last gate mission. It never hits back. Every kill drops 3–8 Hero Tokens.

Corsair Captain (secret boss) Godkiller

Hidden on the map and far tougher than anything else here. You don't need it to unlock the next world. It has a 1% chance per kill to drop the Flaming aura (+15% coins). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 64 Forge Core.
