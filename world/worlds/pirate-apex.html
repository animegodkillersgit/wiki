Bosses

Yonko Guard (gate boss) Sovereign

Beat it 5 times for the last gate mission. It never hits back. Every kill drops 3–8 Hero Tokens.

Emperor's Right-Hand (secret boss) Godkiller

Hidden on the map and far tougher than anything else here. You don't need it to unlock the next world. It has a 1% chance per kill to drop the Statue aura (+75% energy). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 128 Forge Core.

Emperor Goldbraid (sword boss) Godkiller

This boss fights back: a circle warns you 1 seconds before each hit, and it strikes every 3.2 seconds. Step out of the circle to dodge. It is not part of the gate missions. It drops swords: Sea Cutlass (30%), Haki Edge (8%), Abyss Blade (1%). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 32 Forge Core, with a 40% chance of rune fragments.
