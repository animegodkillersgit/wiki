Bosses

Royal Guardsman (gate boss) Sovereign

Beat it 5 times for the last gate mission. It never hits back. Every kill drops 3–8 Hero Tokens.

Emperor's Vanguard (secret boss) Godkiller

Hidden on the map and far tougher than anything else here, with the HP of a Veteran enemy from World 4. You don't need it to unlock the next world. It has a 1% chance per kill to drop the Red Emperor aura (+10% damage). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 8 Forge Core.

Tyrant Frostlord (sword boss) Godkiller

This boss fights back: a circle warns you 1.4 seconds before each hit, and it strikes every 4 seconds. Step out of the circle to dodge. It is not part of the gate missions. It drops swords: Trainee Blade (40%), Nimbus Saber (12%), Spirit Edge (3%). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 2 Forge Core, with a 40% chance of rune fragments.
