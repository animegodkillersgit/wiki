Bosses

Cloak Operative (gate boss) Sovereign

Beat it 5 times for the last gate mission. It never hits back. Every kill drops 3–8 Hero Tokens.

Tailed Guardian (secret boss) Godkiller

Hidden on the map and far tougher than anything else here, with the HP of a Veteran enemy from World 6. You don't need it to unlock the next world. It has a 1% chance per kill to drop the Fire King aura (+25% drop). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 32 Forge Core.

Masked Akato (sword boss) Godkiller

This boss fights back: a circle warns you 1.2 seconds before each hit, and it strikes every 3.6 seconds. Step out of the circle to dodge. It is not part of the gate missions. It drops swords: Kunai Cleaver (35%), Chakra Tanto (10%), Sage Katana (2.5%). Every kill also drops 3–8 Hero Tokens and 8 Forge Core, with a 40% chance of rune fragments.
