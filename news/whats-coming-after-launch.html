What's Coming After Launch

Six worlds are just the start. Here's what we can already share about the road ahead.

World 7 Is Next

  • World 7 will be the next world added to the game, on the way to 20 worlds.
  • If you've finished the World 6 missions, it opens for you right away, with nothing to redo.
  • The Pirate Apex secret boss is built as a late challenge: expect to beat it after World 7 arrives.

What Every New World Brings

  • 7 new enemies, including a gate boss and a secret boss with its own aura
  • A new egg with 7 new pets
  • 2 new token gachas with new tokens to collect
  • Token packs for the new gachas
  • A new world upgrade
  • 6 new gate missions, with a lasting energy and damage reward for finishing the world's quests
  • Higher caps on Coin upgrades
  • New quests and new Index entries

The first prestige also comes within reach in the worlds after World 6.

Along the Way

  • A new code with free Gems and a potion for each update
  • New battle pass seasons, with new generals and auras to earn

Also Planned

  • Biome Tower, a new raid
  • Medium and Hard for Time Attack, Boss Rush, Defense and Endless
  • A second active general
  • More aura slots
  • Boosted rates on gacha banners
World 7's name, theme and release date aren't set yet. Plans can change as the game develops.
