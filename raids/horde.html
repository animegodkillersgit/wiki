Horde is a free raid that opens on a schedule. Clear waves together and earn rune dust for your runes.

How It Works

Waves

A run has 50 waves, and you get 30 seconds for each one. If a wave is not cleared in time, the run ends.

Enemies and Bosses

A normal wave has 5 enemies that split the wave's HP equally. Waves 6, 11, 16 … 46 and 50 have a single boss with all the HP. Bosses hit you. If you die, you respawn and keep fighting.

All Difficulties Are Open

You can enter Easy, Medium and Hard from the start. HP is the same for every player and does not depend on your world.

Shared Run

Everyone who enters a difficulty during its opening on your server plays in one run. Enemies, HP and the timer are shared, and more players do not add HP.

Joining Late

The run starts as soon as the first player enters. If you enter later, you join the current wave, and earlier waves do not count for you.

Full Rewards for Everyone

Each player gets full rewards for every wave and enemy cleared while they are in the run, no matter who got the kill.

Entry

Entry stays open for 1 minute. Each opening has one run. If that run ends early, the difficulty is closed until its next opening.

Coins, XP and Tokens

Every enemy defeated gives coins and XP. Normal enemies split the wave amount, and a boss gives all of it. Your coin and XP bonuses apply. Each enemy also has a 10% chance to drop 1–5 world tokens. The number is boosted by Drops.

Achievements and Leaderboards

Achievements

Every full run, on any difficulty, adds 1 to the Raids achievement. Horde bosses count for the Bosses achievement, and every enemy counts for Enemies Defeated.

Leaderboards

Each difficulty has its own board that counts your full runs. A full run means all 50 waves counted for you, so you must be there from wave 1. Boards cover all servers and all time. There are no prizes.
