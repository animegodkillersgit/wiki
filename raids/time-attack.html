Time Attack

Time Attack is a pure damage test. Hit a training dummy until the timer ends and climb the damage steps.

How It Works

The Dummy

There is one target. It has no HP and never hits you, so you can't die here. A run always lasts the full 60 seconds, even after you reach Diamond.

Playing Together

Your own run starts the moment you enter. Other players on your server can join it from the run list for the first 60 seconds, each with their own key. There is no player limit except the server size.

Your Damage Counts

Everyone hits the same dummy, and the screen shows the run total and your own damage. Only your own damage counts for your achievement and the leaderboard: your hand, pets and traits. If you join late, only damage after you enter counts.

Bonuses

The dummy is not a boss, so bonuses against bosses do not work on it.

More Difficulties

Medium (90 seconds) and Hard (120 seconds) open in later updates, each with its own achievement.

Rewards

Achievement Only

Time Attack gives no coins, XP, EC or tokens. Each step you reach in one run unlocks a level of its achievement, once. Every level adds Damage for good.

Weekly Board

The board shows your best damage in one run. It covers all servers, resets every Monday at 0:00 UTC and has no prizes.
