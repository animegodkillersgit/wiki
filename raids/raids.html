Raids

Raids are separate challenges with their own rewards. Enemy HP is the same for every player, so the stronger you are, the further you get. Big numbers use the letters from the Ranks page.

Horde has all three difficulties open from the start. The other raids only have Easy for now. Medium and Hard open in later updates.

Keys

Horde is free. Every other raid needs a key.

  • Buy keys with EC in the Exchange Shop in the Raid Lobby. A key costs 1,000 EC.
  • Each raid and difficulty has its own key, like the Defense Easy Key.
  • One key is one run, used when you enter. This also goes for joining someone else's run.
  • There is no daily limit, and there are no free keys.

Raid Window

Walk up to a raid entrance to open its window. It shows the difficulties, your best run, the rewards and their chances, and the recommended DPS. Time Attack, Defense and Endless also list the runs on your server. Each run shows the host, the number of players and the current wave, or the time left in Time Attack.

The key button shows how many keys you have. With a key, it starts your run. Without one, it opens the Exchange Shop on that key. Horde has no key button: it shows the time until the next opening and a Join button.

When a Horde difficulty opens, everyone not in a run gets a popup. Press Yes to join from anywhere, or No to close it. You can turn the popup off in the settings.

Recommended DPS

Recommended DPS is the damage per second you need for that raid's first reward or first achievement level. It's only a tip: you can enter with less. Your own DPS is shown on the battle screen.

During a Run

  • The next wave or boss appears 1 second after the last one is cleared.
  • Enemies appear inside your attack radius and stay there, so you never have to chase them.
  • You get rewards during the run. They stay with you however it ends, and there is no results screen.
  • After a run, you are back at the entrance of that raid.
  • The screen shows the raid and difficulty, the wave (like Wave 3/50) with the enemies defeated and the wave timer. Boss Rush shows the boss number and the boss timer, and Time Attack shows the timer and the damage.

Leaving a Run

Press Leave at any time. You leave right away with no "Are you sure?" window. You keep what you earned, but you don't get your key back.

  • The Teleport window also works: pick a world and press Teleport to leave the run and go there.
  • If you disconnect, it counts as leaving. If you are alone, the run ends. In a shared run, you can come back with a new key while entry is open, if you are on the same server.
  • When everyone has left, the run ends.

Playing Together

You can't invite friends and there are no lobbies. A friend on your server sees your run in the list and joins by themselves. Boss Rush is always solo. Your friend must also have unlocked that raid's world.

More players don't add enemy HP, and there is no group bonus.

Auto Retry and Auto Leave

Time Attack, Boss Rush, Defense and Endless have these settings. Each raid has its own, they start off, and they stay saved. You turn them on in the Settings window. Horde doesn't have them.

Auto Retry starts a new run of the same raid and difficulty when a run ends by itself, even if you lost.

  • Each new run uses 1 key.
  • With no keys left, you go back to the entrance. The setting stays on, and the game never buys keys for you.
  • Pressing Leave, using Teleport or disconnecting doesn't start a new run.

Auto Leave makes you leave after a wave you choose. In Boss Rush, you choose a boss. Time Attack doesn't have it. Auto Retry also works after Auto Leave.

In a shared run, the player who opened it is the host.

  • Auto Retry is personal. When a run ends, everyone with it on and a key goes into one new run together, each with their own key.
  • Auto Leave only works for the host. Guests leave with the Leave button.
  • The host's Auto Leave wave is shown in the run list, so you can see it before you join.
  • When the host's Auto Leave starts a new run, players with Auto Retry and a key go along, and everyone else finishes the old run.
  • If the host leaves, the next player who joined becomes the host.

Leaderboards

Every raid has leaderboards across all servers, and none of them have prizes. See them in Hall of Fame. Horde counts full runs for all time. The others show your best run of the week and reset on Monday at 0:00 UTC.
