Coins

Coins are the main currency you earn by fighting. Enemies give coins, and later worlds give more. Boss Rush is the one exception.

  • Buy eggs. See Pets.
  • Buy levels of the 11 coin upgrades. The price only depends on the level, not on where you stand.
  • Trade them for Hero Tokens.

Gems

Gems are the premium currency. Enemies and bosses never drop them.

Free Gems come from daily tasks, weekly tasks, login rewards and codes. If you log in every day and finish every task, that is about 300 Gems a day. Tiers 5 and 10 of the Raids achievement give 1,500 and 3,000 Gems, and every Prestige Box gives 5,000 Gems. Prestige comes after World 6, so you can't get a box yet.

Gem packs are sold for Robux in the Shop. Every pack gives 10 Gems per Robux.

Hero Token

Hero Tokens (HT) level up your generals. They give no power by themselves.

  • Gate bosses, secret bosses and bosses that drop swords each drop 3 to 8 HT per kill, in every world.
  • In Boss Rush, every boss you kill has a 30% chance to drop 1 HT.
  • A general or aura gacha roll that gives no prize pays 200 HT.
  • Tasks give up to 100 HT a day and 700 HT a week.
  • The battle pass gives 100 HT on the free track and 300 HT on the premium track, every fifth level starting at level 1.
  • The VIP chest gives 50 HT a day.
  • You can trade coins for HT, one at a time. One HT costs the coins of 20 average kills in your furthest unlocked world.

X2 Hero Tokens, the Hero Token potion and VIP multiply only the HT from bosses.

Exchange Coin

Exchange Coins (EC) are what you get for world tokens you don't need. They give no power and don't unlock worlds.

  • Trade 1 world token of any type for 1 EC. You choose when to trade.
  • Each daily task gives 30 EC and each weekly task 120 EC.
  • Day 3 of the login rewards gives 30 EC.
  • Apology codes give 100 EC.
  • The battle pass gives 200 EC on the free track and 500 EC on the premium track, every fifth level starting at level 2.

Spend EC at the Exchange Shop in the Raid Lobby.

Battle Token

Battle Tokens (BT) buy inventory space. They give no power.

  • In the Defense raid, every wave you clear has a 50% chance to give 1 BT.
  • Finishing all daily tasks gives 10 BT, and all weekly tasks 50 BT.
  • The battle pass gives 20 BT on the free track and 50 BT on the premium track, every fifth level starting at level 4.

The pet, sword and accessory inventories each have 10 levels. Every level costs 150 BT and adds 10 spaces, so a full inventory costs 1,500 BT.

General Insignia

General Insignia pay for general trait rerolls. One attempt costs 12 Insignia. If the reroll lands on the trait the general already has, you get them back.

Enemies and bosses don't drop Insignia. You get 3 from a general or aura gacha roll that gives no prize, or buy packs with Gems.

Reset Stats

One Reset Stats clears your stat points once, however many you have placed. The price stays the same no matter how many times you reset.

  • One costs 29 R$ or 290 Gems.
  • A set of 100 costs 999 R$.

Enemies, bosses and raids don't drop Reset Stats.

World Tokens

Every world has three types of token: one for each of its two token gachas and one for its world upgrade. In the game, each is named after its gacha or upgrade. They drop from enemies, and the Drop bonus gives you more.

Tokens for the gachas are also sold for Robux at the gacha window. Each pack is for that gacha only.

Robux

Robux (R$) is Roblox money. In this game it buys gamepasses, Gems, the premium battle pass, potions and bundles, Reset Stats and gacha token packs. The premium battle pass costs 449 R$, or 1,999 R$ with 50 levels included.

Hero Tokens, upgrade tokens and other resources are not sold for Robux.
