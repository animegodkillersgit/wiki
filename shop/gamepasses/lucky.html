How it works

What luck does

Luck raises your chance of rare pets from eggs (Champion and higher) and of swords and auras from bosses. It does nothing for gachas, tokens, herbs or crits.

Luck gamepasses add up

Each one is a separate purchase and works on its own. Together their bonuses add up: all three give ×2.75.
