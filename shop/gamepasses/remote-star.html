Remote Star

Remote Star is a gamepass. You can open the egg of any unlocked world from the Teleport window, without traveling there.

399 R$Price
Any unlocked worldWorks in

How it works

Background Auto

Your Auto keeps opening eggs while you fight in any world or raid, until you press Stop. Leaving the game does not turn it off.

Token gachas

Auto at a token gacha keeps rolling in the background too.

One egg at a time

While Auto runs in the background, press Stop before you open a different egg.

Other windows

Everyone can open gachas and stations from the Teleport window. Eggs are the part that needs this gamepass.
