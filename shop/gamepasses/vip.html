How it works

Daily VIP chest

Once per game day: 50 Hero Tokens, 10 world tokens and 1 or 2 size M potions.

What is in the chest

World tokens come from your furthest unlocked world, all of one random type. Each potion is a random type. There are no Gems in the chest.

Chest and bonuses

Bonuses do not change the Hero Tokens and world tokens in the chest.

New chest every day

A new chest appears at 00:00 UTC. Missed days do not stack.
