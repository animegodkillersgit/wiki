Gamepasses

Gamepasses are bonuses you buy with Robux in the Shop window, which you can open from anywhere in the game. There are 19 gamepasses, and each one has its own page.

How Bonuses Work Together

  • The ×2 gamepasses multiply with your other bonuses.
  • Lucky, Super Lucky and Ultra Lucky add together: +25%, +50% and +100%, for ×2.75 with all three.
  • VIP and X2 Energy multiply, for ×2.3 energy per click.
X2 Hero Tokens and VIP boost Hero Tokens from bosses, not those from other sources. See Currencies.
