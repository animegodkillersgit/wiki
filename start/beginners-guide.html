Beginner's Guide

Welcome to Anime Godkillers! The game has no tutorial, so here is what to do first. You start in Kame Foothills, World 1.

1. Pick Your First Pet

On your first login you choose Pup Saiba, Leaf Toad or Crab Hand. They all have the same strength, so take the one you like. It goes straight into a pet slot.

2. Click to Build Energy

Every click does two things: it adds energy and it hits the nearest enemy in range and stays on it until it dies or leaves your range. The more energy you have stored, the harder you hit.

Each click rolls one crit. A crit makes your hit stronger, ×1.5 at first and up to ×5, and gives ×1.2 energy. A crit shows "Critical" above the enemy. Crit chance starts at 5% and can reach 50%.

  • Beaten enemies give coins and XP.
  • With no enemy in range, clicks still give energy but deal no damage.
  • Turn on Auto Pet on the battle screen, and your pets attack the same enemy on their own.

3. Turn On Auto Clicker

The Auto Clicker switch clicks for you, even when you're not near an enemy. It starts switched off, and so does every other auto option.

  • You need to join the game's Roblox group to use Auto Clicker. Press the switch and Roblox will ask you to join.
  • Free Auto Clicker does 1 click per second. The Fast Attack gamepass is ×5.4 faster.
  • While Auto Clicker is on, your own taps don't count.
  • While Auto Clicker is on, a circle around you shows your attack radius.

If Roblox disconnects you for being idle, the game reconnects you in the same spot, with your auto options as they were. In a raid, this counts as a disconnect.

4. Open Eggs

Spend your coins at the World 1 egg. Each egg costs 25 coins and gives you a pet.

  • You start with 3 pet slots. Pets in slots give you energy and hit enemies with you.
  • Feed spare pets to your best pet with Pet Fusion to level it up.

5. Rank Up

Your first Rank Up costs 9,000 energy. It uses up all your stored energy and multiplies your energy per click by 2, forever.

Your hits will be weak right after a Rank Up, but they grow back faster than before. Once you're comfortable, turn on auto Rank Up. See the full table on the Ranks page.

6. Finish the Gate Missions

To unlock World 2, beat each World 1 enemy the set number of times, from Sparring Bot up to the gate boss Armored Elite. That's 83 kills in total, in any order, and your progress is saved. The World 1 Missions tracker on your screen shows how far you are. When you finish, the new world unlocks in your Teleport window.

You don't need to beat the secret boss. Come back for it when you're stronger. Learn more on the Worlds page.

7. Get Stronger

Enemies drop tokens as you fight. Here's how to use what you collect:

  • Tokens: roll the world's token gachas. A roll costs 10 tokens, and the best result you've rolled stays active.
  • Levels: every level gives you stat points (1 at first, more after each prestige) for Energy, Damage, Coins or Luck. Spend them at the stats zone in the Raid Lobby, or from the Teleport window. See Level & Prestige.
  • Coins: buy more eggs and permanent coin upgrades at the coin upgrades zone in the Raid Lobby, or from the Teleport window. Rank Ups don't reset those upgrades.
  • Best gear: the Equip Best buttons put on your strongest set for the stat you choose.

8. Come Back Every Day

  • Do your 5 daily and 5 weekly quests for Gems and other rewards.
  • Collect your login reward. Missing a day doesn't reset the 7-day cycle.
  • Open your daily chest. Join the game's group for a second chest.
  • Try the free Horde raid in the Raid Lobby once you're stronger.
